1 OFDM-MIMO Decoding
Consider an OFDM transmitter sending bit sequence b, which could be 0 or 1 with equal probability.
The transmitter uses BPSK modulation, i.e., it sends −1 if b is 0, and +1 if b is 1. The symbols
are loaded into 64 subcarriers with an appropriate guard band and are converted into time samples
using IFFT, and an appropriate CP is added. Assume that the channel introduces additive Gaussian
random noise with zero mean, and a standard deviation of σ besides channel response. We also use
a legacy OFDM preamble (STS and 2.5 copy of LTS) along with MIMO preamble (One LTS each
per antenna per time-slot for MIMO channel estimation).
1. Case 1: (1x2 MIMO) Consider a case where the transmitter has one antenna, and the
receiver has two antennas, with the received symbol as yi,A = hAxi + ni,A and yi,B = hBxi +
ni,B, where the ni,A and ni,B are i.i.d. random Gaussian variables, with zero mean and
standard deviation of σ. The index i represents time-domain samples. What is the receiver’s
decoding rule that gives the best estimate of bi
, given yi,A, yi,B, hA, hB, and σ? Also, provide
the analytical expression for SNR.
Hint: Use maximum ratio combining.
2. Case 2: (1x4 MIMO) Consider a case where the transmitter has one antenna, and the
receiver has four antennas with the received symbol as yi,A = hAxi + ni,A, yi,B = hBxi + ni,B,
yi,C = hCxi + ni,C, yi,D = hDxi + ni,D where ni,A, ni,B, ni,C, ni,D are i.i.d. random Gaussian
variables, with zero mean and standard deviation of σ. What is the receiver’s decoding rule
that gives the best estimate of bi
, given yi,A, yi,B, yi,C, yi,D, hA, hB, hC, hD and σ? Provide the
analytical expression for SNR and compare it with the previous case 1, which one is better?
3. Case 3: (2x1 MIMO) Consider a case where transmitter has two antenna and is only
transmitting one stream like signal, and the receiver has one antenna, with the received
symbol as yi = hAxi,A + hBxi,B + ni
, where ni
is a random Gaussian variable, with zero
mean and standard deviation of σ. The transmit precoding implements xi,A = ci,Axi and
xi,B = ci,Bxi
, where xi
is a single stream of data (indexed by time sample i), and ci,A, ci,B
are precoding coefficients. What is the transmitter’s precoding / receiver’s decoding rule that
gives the best estimate of bi
, given yi
, hA, hB, and σ? Provide the analytical expression for
SNR and compare it with the expressions from Case 1 and Case 2; which one is better?
